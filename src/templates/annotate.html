🏷️ Annotation Tool - Click & Drag to Draw

Instructions: Select a product, then click and drag to draw bounding boxes. Draw multiple boxes for products with quantity > 1.
Keyboard Shortcuts: S=Save | D=Delete Selected Box | Shift+D=Delete All Boxes for Product | N=Next | P=Previous
To Delete: Click on a box to select it (turns red), then press D to delete that specific box
